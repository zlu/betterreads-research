# BetterReads Research

Citeable methodology and a small literary annotation sample for **AI-assisted reading of difficult books**.

BetterReads is an AI-assisted literary reading platform that helps readers understand difficult books through structured semantic annotation, contextual explanation, and interactive literary knowledge.

**Site:** [betterreads.dev/research](https://www.betterreads.dev/research)  
**App:** [betterreads.dev](https://www.betterreads.dev)

This repository is the public research package. It is not the BetterReads product source.

## Dataset v0.1

A chapter-scoped sample from Joseph Conrad’s *Heart of Darkness* (Standard Ebooks edition, Part I / `0000-i`).

**Included:** public-domain chapter text; characters; themes; timeline events; glossary terms attested in the chapter; relationship subgraph; theme×chapter scores.

**Start here:** [`literary-annotation-v0.1/samples/heart_of_darkness_se/sample.json`](literary-annotation-v0.1/samples/heart_of_darkness_se/sample.json)

**Schema:** [`literary-annotation-v0.1/schema/literary-annotation.schema.json`](literary-annotation-v0.1/schema/literary-annotation.schema.json)
