# AI Reading Companions

**Author:** Zhao Lu  
**Date:** 2026-10-04  
**Canonical URL:** https://www.betterreads.dev/research/ai-reading-companions

## Abstract

An AI reading companion in BetterReads is a chapter-aligned assistance layer that uses structured annotation (characters, themes, glossary, timeline) to keep readers oriented inside a difficult book. The v0.1 public sample publishes the structured substrate; companion prose lives in the reader.

## Definition

A reading companion is not a book summary app. It is contextual help attached to a specific chapter of a specific edition: who matters now, which terms block comprehension, which themes intensify, what just happened in narrative time.

## Companions vs the research sample

Companion prose (chapter summaries, paraphrases, extended historical notes) is experienced in the BetterReads reader. The research sample publishes the structured substrate—entities and chapter alignment—so the annotation model can be inspected and cited on its own.

## Evaluation intuition

Useful companions should increase orientation and finish rates on hard texts while remaining falsifiable against the chapter text. Structured layers (glossary attestation, timeline chapter_id, theme scores) make that check possible; unconstrained chat does not.
